Help & Policies

Shipping & Delivery

We deliver across Jordan. Orders of 50 JOD or more receive free delivery; otherwise delivery is 2 JOD. Expected delivery is within 24–48 hours. We will contact you to confirm your delivery details.

Returns & Refunds

If there is a problem with your order, please contact TEEB Perfumes as soon as possible using the details on our Contact page. Return or replacement eligibility depends on the condition of the product and the circumstances of the order.

Terms & Conditions

Product availability is subject to stock. Orders are confirmed after checkout and may be followed by contact from TEEB Perfumes. Payment is currently Cash on Delivery. Product details, prices and availability may be updated when necessary.

Privacy Policy

Information submitted during checkout is used to process and deliver your order and communicate with you about it. Please do not submit sensitive information that is not required to fulfill your order.
